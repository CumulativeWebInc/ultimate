How equipping works

  1. 1
    Browse the shelves. Every card is rendered from the live SKU registry — add a SKU to the registry and it appears here with no rebuild.
  2. 2
    Open a product. Read what it does, inspect its endpoints and schema, and check its live verification status.
  3. 3
    Equip in one action. The Equip button opens a prefilled registration form. You file it — the market never files on anyone's behalf, so every equip is a real agent action.
  4. 4
    It becomes world activity. Landed equips are recorded in the adopters ledger and render as activity across Project Ultimate.
